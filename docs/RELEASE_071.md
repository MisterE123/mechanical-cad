## Checked Assembly STEP

Export a complete passing saved pose with one named Assembly, one reusable
Part definition per referenced Part ID, and named instances at their evaluated
canonical placements. Repeated instances share their definition; distinct Part
IDs remain distinct. Export freshly replays accepted Parts and repeats the active
preset's saved-pose check before replacing the explicit destination atomically.
Failed or incomplete checks leave that destination unchanged. Saved PASS labels
cannot authorize export after reopening: Replay and Check Pose are required.

STEP preserves exchange geometry, names and placements. Editable feature history,
joints, verification presets and stable CAD identities stay in `.blend` or
Assembly JSON. Parts are not fused. There is no Assembly STL, nested Assembly or
closed-loop mate solver. A saved-pose check does not prove continuous collision
freedom or certify physical fit, load capacity or manufacturing suitability.
See [the workflow and limits](ASSEMBLY_STEP_EXPORT.md).
