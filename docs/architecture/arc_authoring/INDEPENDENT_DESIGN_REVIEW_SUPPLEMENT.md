   picker and sends its chosen PointRef directly through the ordinary detached
   point-input/proposal/checkpoint-CAS path. It covers initial and later Line/
   Arc phases without cursor repicking, synthetic clicks, snapping or implicit
   constraints. Inspector selection remains selection-only. Phase/source/
   revision/slot/owner/backend bindings, missing or wrong-role identities,
   repeated arc roles, double acceptance and Cancel are explicit. Retained or
   resumed proposals require inactive pointer capture and do not revive it.
   P11/G03 now require the corresponding negative and installed-GUI controls,
   including coincident distinct IDs and successful exact-ID shared closure.
   This direct-picker form satisfies the original finding without enabling
   unrelated inspector mutators during a proposal.
2. The native-failing DoF5 witness now enters through a predeclared pure saved
   draft with recorded base/witness/envelope identity, ordinary Resume and
   genuine Check/Finish. No fixture restores authority, and failed canonical
   file loading remains nonpublishing. N05/G06/E04 explicitly distinguish this
   negative recovery setup from the mandatory manually drawn positive workflow.
