# 1.27.0 Linux reference and usability checkpoint

Status: preparation only. No installer, native or GUI success is claimed here.
The exact frozen source and final qualification receipt will identify completed
checks separately. This is a separate successor to immutable Linux 1.26.

## Scope

- Explicit simple and mixed integer fractions require supported lowercase unit
  suffixes. Bare arithmetic formulas and unchanged source precision/types remain
  unchanged. Pending Line length and Circle diameter accept 1/8in as 3.175 mm;
  Circle stores radius 1.5875 mm once. Invalid 1/0in stays correctable raw intent.
  See [typed unit input](UNIT_INPUT.md).
- Use Solid Edge admits exact planar circular intervals with unique stable face
  and endpoint signatures. Capability-gated opt-in catalogs and canonical arc
  references are explicit; legacy catalogs remain unchanged. Minor/major sweep,
  target-normal reversal and explicit endpoint flip preserve branch and target
  identity. Radius/size edits replay from fresh native evidence. Ordinary local
  construction arcs do not inherit fillet intent. Oblique and ambiguous identity
  cases fail closed. See [solid arc projection](SOLID_ARC_PROJECTION.md).
- Restore Sharp Corner may retire its exact historical radius label as UI-only
  metadata. Intentional retirement is not a geometry warning. Other missing or
  conflicting bindings still warn. Exact layout survives local/Part Undo/Redo,
  Finish and reopen without restoring geometry or native acceptance authority.
  New Restore operations mark their retired labels; older unmarked missing
  labels are not automatically reclassified. The marker describes UI history,
  not proof of producer provenance or geometry.
  See [annotation layout](SKETCH_ANNOTATION_LAYOUT.md).

## Compatibility

Fractions normalize into existing canonical numbers, without a new formula or
canonical schema. Arc reference rows/opcodes require the matching capability;
old strict readers reject them. Old edge catalogs retain their previous records.
Retirement extends scene annotation envelope version 2 and draft UI2 metadata;
old readers reject it. Legacy stores remain version 1 until explicitly promoted.
Retirement does not change the canonical geometry schema or reserve old IDs.
Raw drafts/recovery do not restore solver authority: fresh validation is required.

## Planned bounded qualification

Exact-installed archive checks will verify source/runtime provenance, every
runtime byte and executable mode, registration, sidecar-only OCP/slvs, focused
fraction correction and raw reopen, source-bound arc picking and replay, and
retirement lifecycle. Guarded native results, not float32 display fields, are
required for precise solid statistics. Actual mouse/keyboard GUI observations
will be reported separately from scripted fixtures and direct operator checks.

This is not broad historical-suite, exhaustive GUI, Windows, arbitrary topology
identity, manufacturing or dependency-source/legal qualification. Optional local
recovery is OFF by default, unencrypted and best effort. The intermittent native
allocator crash remains unresolved. No Windows test host, public upload or publication is
part of this local preparation. Prior archives and tags remain unchanged.
