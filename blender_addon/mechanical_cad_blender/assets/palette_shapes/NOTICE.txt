These native derivatives expand strokes and dashes, union paths, flatten curves,
flip Y, quantize to 8-bit coordinates, and tessellate holes into simple polygons.
They are modified representations, not unchanged upstream SVG paths. The source
SVGs, original CAD geometry and upstream Google SVGs remain in ../icons/.
