Original Mechanical CAD toolbar glyphs

The geometry in ../../tool_glyphs.py and its generated PNG and SVG representations
were created for Mechanical CAD by Mechanical CAD contributors and are licensed
GPL-3.0-or-later, matching the project. See the repository LICENSE and
../icons/licenses/GPL-3.0-or-later.txt. No proprietary or Google artwork was traced
or incorporated into these glyphs.

scripts/build_tool_glyphs.py deterministically renders menu previews from the same
bounded polygons used for native Blender button shapes. design_manifest.json
records the four targeted new/revised CAD designs and generated file hashes.
SVG exports use currentColor and the same 24-unit geometry, with Y flipped for
SVG coordinates. Native button drawing additionally insets artwork to 72% of the
hit area; these SVG exports do not change hit targets, names, or tooltips.
